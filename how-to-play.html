How to play

RewindChess uses the normal rules of chess, including check, castling, promotion and en passant. On top of that, you get rewinds and, in one mode, a special move.

1. Choose a mode

Mode Rewinds per player Special moves per player Best for
Practice50Learning and trying ideas
Competitive10A serious game
Competitive + Abilities11A game with one surprise each

2. Rewind rules

Looking is free

Click any move in the history or any timeline to view it. You cannot move pieces while viewing.

Playing on costs one rewind

Press “Continue from here” on your turn. Going back 1 move or 20 moves costs the same: one rewind.

No refunds

A used rewind stays used, even if you go back further or switch to another timeline.
